<h2>1. So What Exactly Is Affiliate Marketing?</h2>

<p>Affiliate marketing is when you earn a commission for recommending someone else's product or service. Here's the simple version:</p>

<p><strong>3 parties involved:</strong></p>

<ol>
<li><strong>Merchant</strong> — Amazon, GreenGeeks, HostAfrica, etc. They have the product.</li>
<li><strong>Affiliate</strong> — That's you. You promote it.</li>
<li><strong>Customer</strong> — The person who buys.</li>
</ol>

<p>You don't handle shipping, customer service, or inventory. You just send traffic.</p>

<p><strong>Example:</strong> I write a review of the <a href="https://amzn.to/3P6IXPy">Logitech C920 webcam</a>. You click my Amazon link and buy it. Amazon pays me ~$3. That's affiliate marketing.</p>

<p>When someone clicks your link, a small file called a <strong>cookie</strong> is saved in their browser. If they buy within the cookie window (usually 30–90 days), you earn the commission — even if they close the browser and come back later. No technical knowledge needed. The company tracks everything automatically.</p>

<p>You need: 10 blog posts, an About page, a Privacy page, and a Disclosure page.</p>

<h3>Step 3: Write "Money Posts"</h3>
<p>These 3 post types make 90% of affiliate income:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Reviews</strong> — "Logitech C920 Webcam Review"</li>
<li><strong>Comparisons</strong> — "<a href="https://www.kapyamarket.co.za/2026/05/greengeeks-vs-hostinger-2026.html">GreenGeeks vs Hostinger</a>"</li>

</ul>
<p>Each post needs 2–3 affiliate links. Always add <code>rel="sponsored nofollow"</code> to every affiliate link — this is required by Google.</p>

<h3>Step 4: Get Traffic</h3>
<p>No traffic = no sales. Start with Pinterest — it's free and fast.</p>
<ol>
<li>Make 3 pins per blog post</li>
<li>Post to 5 boards</li>
<li>Target keywords: "best webcam for blogging," "how to start affiliate marketing"</li>
</ol>
<p>Also answer questions on Quora and Reddit — drop 1 helpful link per answer maximum.</p>

<h3>Step 5: Track &amp; Improve</h3>
<p>Amazon Associates → Reports. Check clicks daily. If 100 people click but 0 buy, your post needs work. If 10 clicks = 1 sale, write more posts like that.</p>

<h2>5. The Biggest Mistake Beginners Make</h2>

<p>They try to promote everything.</p>

<p>One week it's fitness gear. Next week it's crypto. Next week it's cooking equipment. Google can't rank you. Readers don't trust you. Commissions stay at zero.</p>

<p><strong>Pick one niche. Promote 2–3 products you actually believe in. Write 10 honest posts. Then wait.</strong></p>

<p>The bloggers who make money aren't the most talented. They're the ones who didn't quit in month 2.</p>
