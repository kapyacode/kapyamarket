<h2>Affiliate Link Management</h2>

<h3>5. Affiliate Cloaker</h3>
<ul>
<li>Masks long ugly affiliate links into clean short URLs</li>
<li>Tracks clicks so you know what's converting</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Best for:</strong> Clean, professional-looking links that build trust.</p>

<h3>6. PrettyLinks (Free)</h3>
<ul>
<li>Link management and click tracking</li>
<li>Auto-links keywords throughout your posts</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Best for:</strong> Organising many affiliate links across a growing blog.</p>

<h2>Security &amp; Backups</h2>

</ul>

<h2>What NOT to Install</h2>

<ul>
<li><strong>Too many plugins</strong> — every plugin adds load time. Only install what you actually use.</li>
<li><strong>Pirated/nulled premium plugins</strong> — huge security risk and no support when things break.</li>
<li><strong>Plugins not updated in 12+ months</strong> — outdated plugins are a common entry point for hackers.</li>
</ul>
