<h2>Top 6 Shopify Features for Beginners</h2>

<h3>1. Drag and Drop Store Builder</h3>
<p>No coding needed. Pick a theme, drag your products in, and your store is live. Most beginners have their first store up within a few hours.</p>

<h3>2. 100+ Free and Paid Themes</h3>
<p>Shopify has professionally designed themes for every niche — fashion, electronics, food, digital products, and more. Free themes look great and are mobile-optimized out of the box.</p>

<h3>3. Built-in Payment Processing</h3>
<p>Shopify Payments lets you accept credit cards, debit cards, and PayPal without needing a separate payment gateway. South African stores can also integrate <strong>PayFast</strong> and <strong>Peach Payments</strong> for local ZAR payments.</p>

<h3>4. App Store With 8,000+ Apps</h3>
<p>Add any feature you need—email marketing, upsells, loyalty programs, reviews, and more—through the Shopify App Store. Many apps have free plans.</p>

<h3>5. Mobile App</h3>
<p>Manage your entire store from your phone. Process orders, check sales, and respond to customers during load shedding using your mobile data.</p>

<h3>6. Built-in SEO Tools</h3>
<p>Shopify automatically generates sitemaps, lets you edit meta titles and descriptions, and creates clean URLs — making it easier for Google to rank your store.</p>

<h2>Shopify for South Africans — What You Need to Know</h2>

<p>Shopify works in South Africa, but there are a few things to be aware of:</p>

<ul>
  <li>💳 <strong>Shopify Payments is not available in SA</strong>—you will need a third-party payment gateway like PayFast, Peach Payments, or PayGate</li>
  <li>💵 <strong>Pricing is in USD</strong> — budget for exchange rate fluctuations</li>
  <li>🚚 <strong>Shipping integrations</strong> — you can connect local couriers like The Courier Guy and Aramex South Africa</li>
  <li>📦 <strong>Dropshipping works well</strong>—many South African Shopify store owners use dropshipping to sell without holding stock</li>
</ul>

<h2>How to Make Money With Shopify as a Beginner</h2>

<p>There are 3 main ways South African beginners use Shopify to earn online:</p>

<h3>1. Dropshipping</h3>
<p>Sell products without holding stock. When a customer orders, your supplier ships directly to them. You keep the profit margin. Use apps like DSers or Zendrop to find products.</p>

<h3>2. Selling Digital Products</h3>
<p>Sell eBooks, templates, courses, or printables. No shipping, no stock, no courier headaches. Perfect for South Africans who want pure online income.</p>

<h3>3. Print on Demand</h3>
<p>Design T-shirts, mugs, and phone cases. A print-on-demand supplier prints and ships when someone orders. Apps like Printful integrate directly with Shopify.</p>
