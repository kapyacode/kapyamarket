<h2>Mistake #1: Trying to Sell to Everyone</h2>

<p><strong>The mistake:</strong> "My product is for anyone who wants to make money online!" No. If you sell to everyone, you sell to no one.</p>

<p><strong>Why it fails in SA:</strong> Facebook ads cost R15–40 per click here. If your message is vague, you burn money fast. I spent R800 on ads targeting "18–65, South Africa" and got 0 leads.</p>

<p><strong>The fix: Pick 1 person.</strong></p>

<ul>
<li><strong>Bad:</strong> "I help people make money."</li>
<li><strong>Good:</strong> "I help SA moms start affiliate blogs for R0."</li>
</ul>

<p>My site targets beginners in SA who want to do <a href="https://www.kapyamarket.co.za/2026/05/what-is-affiliate-marketing-complete-guide.html">affiliate marketing</a>. Specific = cheaper ads, higher conversions.</p>

<hr/>

<h2>Mistake #2: No Website — Only Social Media</h2>

<p><strong>The mistake:</strong> "I'll just use Instagram and TikTok." Then Instagram bans you. Or the algorithm dies. Your whole business vanishes overnight.</p>

<p><strong>Real SA example:</strong> In 2024, thousands of SA creators lost income when TikTok was nearly banned. No email list = no business.</p>

<p><strong>The fix: Own your traffic.</strong></p>

<ol>
<li><strong>Start a free blog</strong> — Blogger, like Kapya Market. You control it.</li>
<li><strong>Collect emails</strong> — Use MailerLite free plan. 1,000 subscribers = R5,000/month potential.</li>
<li><strong>Social = traffic source,</strong> not your business. Send followers to your blog.</li>
</ol>

<p>You need hosting, a privacy page, and an about page. Takes 1 day to set up.</p>

<h2>Mistake #4: Spending Money on Ads Before Getting 1 Organic Sale</h2>

<p><strong>The mistake:</strong> "I'll run R500 Facebook ads to get sales." You get clicks but 0 sales because your offer hasn't been tested. Now you're R500 poorer.</p>

<p><strong>SA reality:</strong> R500 = 2 weeks of groceries. Don't gamble it.</p>

<p><strong>The fix: Prove it works for free first.</strong></p>

<ol>
<li>Write 3 blog posts with affiliate links</li>
<li>Post on Pinterest — free SA traffic. Search "blogging tips South Africa."</li>
<li>Answer questions on Quora/Reddit — drop your link when genuinely helpful</li>
<li>Get 3 organic sales — now you know your offer converts</li>
</ol>

<p>Only then spend R100 on ads to scale. I follow this rule. It has saved me thousands.</p>

<h2>Mistake #5: No Video Content</h2>

<p><strong>The mistake:</strong> "I'm shy. I'll just write." In 2026, Google prioritizes sites with video. People trust faces, not faceless blogs.</p>

<p><strong>The fix: Start with an R0 setup.</strong></p>

<p>You don't need a studio. You need:</p>

<ul>
<li><strong>Webcam:</strong> <a href="https://amzn.to/3P6IXPy">Logitech C920</a> — R1,200 on Amazon. 1080p, autofocus, dual mics built in.</li>
<li><strong>Light:</strong> Sit near a window. Free.</li>
<li><strong>Mic:</strong> The C920 has dual mics built in — no extra cost.</li>
</ul>

<p>Record 1-minute videos — "3 mistakes I made" — and embed them in your blog posts. My traffic doubled when I added video.</p>

<p><strong>No budget?</strong> Use your phone. Upload to YouTube. Embed in your blog. That's what I did in month 1.</p>
