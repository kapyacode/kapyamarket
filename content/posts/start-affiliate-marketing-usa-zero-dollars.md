<h2>What Is Affiliate Marketing? (30-Second Version)</h2>

<p>You recommend a product online. Someone clicks your unique link and buys it. You earn a commission. That is the whole business model.</p>

<p>You never touch the product. The brand handles stock, shipping, and returns. Your only job is connecting the right South African buyer with the right product at the right moment.</p>

<h2>Step 4: The 90-Day Consistency Blueprint</h2>

<h3>Rule 1: Write Like a Friend, Not a Salesman</h3>
<p>"I personally use HostAfrica for my SA blog and here is my honest experience" converts far better than "Buy HostAfrica now." People buy from people they trust.</p>

<h3>Rule 2: Focus on the Problem, Not the Product</h3>
<p>"Struggling with slow website speeds in Johannesburg?" brings in readers. A product name alone does not.</p>

<h3>Rule 3: Post 3 Times a Week for 90 Days Straight</h3>
<p>After 90 days of consistency your Google traffic will start climbing. Most people quit at day 30. Don't be one of them.</p>
