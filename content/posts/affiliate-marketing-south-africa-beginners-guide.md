Affiliate marketing is a way to recommend products or services online. When someone uses your special tracking link and completes a qualifying action, such as a purchase, the company may pay you a commission.

It is not a guaranteed way to make money. It takes time to learn, create useful content, build an audience and earn trust. This guide explains the basics and the questions South African beginners should answer before they start.

## How affiliate marketing works

Most affiliate programmes follow a similar process:

1. You join an affiliate programme or network.
2. You receive a unique tracking link for a product, service or offer.
3. You create helpful content that explains who the product may suit.
4. A reader clicks your link and completes a qualifying action.
5. If the programme approves the conversion, you may earn a commission.

Commission rules vary. Some programmes pay per sale, while others pay per lead, trial, subscription or another action. Always read the official terms before you promote anything.

## Is it possible in South Africa?

Often, yes. Many programmes accept South African applicants, but eligibility, payment methods, minimum payouts and tax rules differ, so you have to check each one yourself.

Before you apply, check:

- Whether the programme accepts South African residents
- Whether it accepts your website, YouTube channel or social media account
- Which countries or audiences it allows you to promote to
- The commission structure and how long the tracking cookie lasts
- The minimum payout and the payment methods available
- Whether you need a bank account or a payment service such as PayPal or Payoneer
- The rules on email marketing, paid advertising and coupon sites

Do not rely on second-hand information. Check the official programme page and its terms.

## Choose one audience

A common beginner mistake is trying to promote everything to everyone. Start with one audience and one problem you understand. For a longer walkthrough, read [how to choose your first affiliate marketing niche](/2026/05/choose-first-affiliate-marketing-niche/).

Examples:

- Students who want low-cost ways to learn digital skills
- Small business owners who need simple website or marketing tools
- South African beginners who want to start a blog on a limited budget
- People comparing web hosting, email tools or AI writing tools

Choose a topic you can research and explain honestly. You do not need to be an expert on day one, but you should be willing to learn and to tell readers the limits of what you know.

## Choose affiliate programmes carefully

Do not choose a programme only because it offers a high commission. A programme can be a poor fit if its rules are unclear, its product does not match your readers or you cannot receive payment from it.

Use this checklist:

| Question | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Does it accept South African affiliates? | Some programmes have country restrictions. |
| Does the product match your audience? | Irrelevant promotions reduce trust and sales. |
| Are the terms clear? | You need to know which actions earn a commission. |
| Can you receive payment? | Confirm the payout methods and minimum thresholds. |
| Is the company reputable? | Check its official site, support pages and independent feedback. |
| Do you understand the product? | You should be able to explain its benefits and its limits. |

## Create a simple website or blog

A blog gives you a place to publish guides, comparisons and tutorials. You can use a simple Hugo site like this one, or a free platform such as Blogger or WordPress.com. Our guide on [how to start a blog](/2026/05/how-to-start-blog-make-money-2026/) walks through the steps.

Before you spend money, decide:

- Your main topic
- Your target reader
- The pages you need: Home, Start Here, About, Contact, Privacy Policy and Affiliate Disclosure
- Your publishing schedule
- Your budget for a domain, hosting and optional tools

A domain and hosting often have introductory prices, but renewal prices can be higher. Check the full first-year and renewal costs in rand before you buy. If you are comparing hosts, see [our guide to web hosting in South Africa](/2026/05/best-hosting-south-africa/).

## Create useful content

Your content should help someone make a decision or finish a task. Instead of writing "Best AI tool", write something specific, such as "Which AI writing tool suits a South African student on a small budget?".

Useful article formats include:

- Beginner guides
- Step-by-step tutorials
- Honest product comparisons
- Tool reviews based on real use
- Frequently asked questions
- Checklists and templates
- South Africa specific guides on pricing, payments and eligibility

For every recommendation, explain:

- Who the product is for
- What problem it solves
- What it costs
- What its limits are
- Who may need a different option
- Where readers can find the official information

## Use affiliate links responsibly

Always disclose affiliate relationships clearly. The disclosure should appear before or near the affiliate links, not hidden at the bottom of the page.

An example disclosure:

> Some links on this page may be affiliate links. If you use one and complete a qualifying purchase, Kapya Market may earn a commission at no extra cost to you.

Use descriptive link text. Instead of "click here", write "see the official pricing page" or "compare the hosting plans". Mark affiliate links with `rel="sponsored nofollow"` where your platform allows it.

Do not make claims you cannot support. Avoid phrases such as "guaranteed income", "get rich quick" or "this will change your life". Only say you tested a product if you did.

## Get traffic without paying for ads

Beginners often focus on affiliate links and forget to help people. Concentrate on content that answers real questions.

Ways to attract visitors include:

- **Search:** write clear titles, headings and direct answers to specific questions.
- **Internal links:** connect related guides on your site.
- **YouTube:** turn a written guide into a short tutorial.
- **Social media:** share useful tips, not only affiliate links.
- **Email:** offer a useful checklist in exchange for an email address.
- **Communities:** take part where it is allowed, and do not spam links.

Search visibility takes time. A new site may not rank quickly for competitive terms, so start with specific, helpful topics. To see what is working, read [how to set up Google Analytics and Search Console](/2026/11/google-analytics-search-console-new-blog/).

## Understand payments and records

Affiliate income is usually paid after a qualifying sale is approved and any refund or cancellation period has passed. Programmes differ on minimum balances, fees, currency conversion and payment dates, so read each programme's payment terms.

Keep records of:

- The programmes you joined
- Your affiliate links and campaigns
- Clicks, sales and commissions
- Payment dates and amounts
- Invoices, receipts and business expenses
- Anything you may need for tax

Tax treatment depends on your situation. For tax advice, speak to a qualified tax practitioner or check the official SARS guidance.

## A realistic first-month plan

- **Week 1:** choose one audience and write down 10 questions they ask.
- **Week 2:** set up or improve your website. Create the essential pages and write your first helpful guide.
- **Week 3:** research two or three relevant affiliate programmes. Read their terms and confirm the payment options.
- **Week 4:** publish another useful article, add internal links and see which content gets attention.

Do not try to publish 30 articles in one month. Consistency and quality matter more than volume.

## Common beginner mistakes

- Promoting products you do not understand
- Copying other websites instead of adding your own experience
- Using misleading headlines or exaggerated income claims
- Ignoring programme rules
- Not disclosing affiliate relationships
- Buying expensive tools before you have created useful content
- Giving up after a few weeks
- Focusing only on sales instead of helping readers

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I need a website?

No, but a website gives you more control over your content and links. Some people start with YouTube, TikTok, Instagram or a newsletter. Whatever you choose, check that the programme allows that channel.

### How much can beginners earn?

There is no reliable fixed amount. Results depend on your topic, traffic, content quality, how much your audience trusts you and how well the offer fits. Many beginners earn nothing at first.

### Can I start with no money?

You can start learning and writing with free tools. A custom domain, hosting and some tools cost money, so build slowly and only pay for what you need.

### How long does it take?

It varies. Many beginners need months of steady learning, publishing and improving before they see meaningful results.

## Final thought

Affiliate marketing can be a useful way to learn digital marketing and build an online presence. Start small, choose one audience and be honest about what you recommend.
