  <p>Choosing the right domain name can make a big difference. Follow these simple tips:</p>

  <p>A short domain name is easier for visitors to type, share and remember.</p>
  <ul>
    <li>Better: <strong>YourBusiness.co.za</strong></li>
    <li>Avoid: <strong>YourBusinessOnlineStore2026.co.za</strong></li>
  </ul>

  <h3 style="color: #0b3d91;">2. Use your brand or business name</h3>
  <p>Your domain should represent what your website is about. If possible, use your business name or a keyword related to your service.</p>

  <p>Numbers and hyphens can confuse visitors and make your domain harder to share verbally.</p>

    <li><strong>.africa</strong>: African brands</li>
