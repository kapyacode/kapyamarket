<h2>1. What Is Affiliate Marketing?</h2>

<p>Affiliate marketing is simple: You recommend a product. Someone clicks your special link. They buy. You get paid.</p>

<p><strong>Example:</strong> I write a review of the <a href="https://amzn.to/4ti0K45">Logitech C920 webcam</a>. You click my Amazon link and buy it. Amazon pays me ~$3. That's affiliate marketing.</p>

<p><strong>3 parties involved:</strong></p>

<ol>
<li><strong>Merchant</strong> — Amazon, GreenGeeks, HostAfrica, etc. They have the product.</li>
<li><strong>Affiliate</strong> — That's you. You promote it.</li>
<li><strong>Customer</strong> — The person who buys.</li>
</ol>

<p>You don't handle shipping, customer service, or inventory. You just send traffic.</p>

<h2>3. How to Start Affiliate Marketing in 2026</h2>

<p>Follow this 5-step plan. Don't skip steps:</p>

<h3>Step 1: Pick 1 Niche</h3>
<p>Don't be "general." Pick 1 topic. Examples: "blogging for moms," "budget gaming setups," "plant care." I picked "affiliate marketing for beginners."</p>

<h3>Step 2: Launch a Simple Blog</h3>
<p>You need a home base. Use Blogger like this site — it's free. Or use WordPress with real hosting if you have a budget.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>South African traffic?</strong> Use <a href="https://my.hostafrica.com/aff.php?aff=3166">HostAfrica</a> — local Johannesburg servers, pay by EFT, from R49/month.</li>
<li><strong>Global traffic (USA, UK, AU)?</strong> Use <a href="https://www.greengeeks.com/track/kapyamarket">GreenGeeks</a> — $2.95/month, 300% green energy, 6 global data centers.</li>
</ul>
<p>You need: 10 blog posts, an About page, a Privacy page, and a Disclosure page.</p>

<h3>Step 3: Write "Money Posts"</h3>
<p>These 3 post types make 90% of affiliate income:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Reviews</strong> — "Logitech C920 Webcam Review"</li>
<li><strong>Comparisons</strong> — "<a href="https://www.kapyamarket.co.za/2026/05/greengeeks-vs-hostinger-2026.html">GreenGeeks vs Hostinger</a>"</li>
<li><strong>Listicles</strong> — "7 Tools I Use to Blog"</li>
</ul>
<p>Each post needs 2–3 affiliate links. Always add <code>rel="sponsored nofollow"</code> to every affiliate link — this is required by Google.</p>

<h3>Step 4: Get Traffic</h3>
<p>No traffic = no sales. Start with Pinterest — it's free and fast.</p>
<ol>
<li>Make 3 pins per blog post</li>
<li>Post to 5 boards</li>
<li>Target keywords: "best webcam for blogging," "how to start affiliate marketing"</li>
</ol>
<p>Also answer questions on Quora and Reddit — drop 1 helpful link per answer maximum.</p>
