<h2>How to choose a good domain name</h2>
<ul>
  <li>Keep it short and easy to spell. If you have to spell it out over the phone, it is too complicated.</li>
  <li>Avoid hyphens and numbers, which people often forget or mistype.</li>
  <li>Use .co.za if your visitors and customers are in South Africa. It signals a local presence straight away.</li>
  <li>Choose .africa if you serve people across the continent but still want an African identity.</li>
  <li>Think about growth. You can add a .com or other extensions later under the same account.</li>
</ul>

<h2>What happens if you forget to renew?</h2>
<p>HostAfrica emails reminders before a domain expires. If you still miss the date, the domain usually enters a short grace period where you can renew, sometimes for an extra fee. After that it may move into a redemption period, where recovery costs more and is not guaranteed. Eventually it can be released to the public, and anyone can register it, including a competitor. Switching on auto-renewal avoids all of that.</p>
