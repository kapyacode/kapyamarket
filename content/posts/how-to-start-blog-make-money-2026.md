<h2>Step 4: Get Indexed in Google — Day 1</h2>

<p>If Google can't find you, you get 0 traffic. Do this after each post:</p>

<ol>
  <li><strong>Google Search Console:</strong> Add your site → Verify → Submit your sitemap.</li>
  <li><strong>URL Inspection:</strong> Paste your new post URL → Click "Request Indexing." This cuts the 14-day wait to 24–72 hours.</li>
  <li><strong>Internal linking:</strong> Every new post must link to 2 old posts. Every old post must link back to the new one. Google follows links to discover content.</li>
</ol>

<p>My posts got traffic in 5 days because I did this. Skip it and you'll wait weeks for any views.</p>
