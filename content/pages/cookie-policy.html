This Cookie Policy explains how Kapya Market ("we", "us") uses cookies and similar technologies on https://www.kapyamarket.co.za/.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites work, remember choices and measure how links perform.

Cookies and similar technologies on this site

  • Essential and security. Our hosting and security provider, Cloudflare, may set technical cookies or use similar technologies to keep the site fast, secure and protected against abuse.
  • Your display choice. If you switch between light and dark mode, your browser may store that choice on your device so the site remembers it.
  • Analytics. We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site, such as which pages are viewed, the type of device and approximate location. It sets cookies (for example, _ga) and processes data such as your IP address under Google's Privacy Policy. You can opt out with the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.
  • Affiliate links. Some links on this site go to partners such as Amazon Associates, HostAfrica and GreenGeeks. When you click one, the partner may place a cookie on your device to record that you came from our site. This is how we earn a commission at no extra cost to you. These cookies are controlled by the partner and governed by its own privacy policy.
  • Email signup forms. If you subscribe through our signup form, it is provided by MailerLite, which may use cookies or similar technologies to run the form and prevent spam.
  • Fonts. Our pages load fonts from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address when the font is requested.
  • Links to other platforms. Links to Facebook, X and YouTube take you to those sites, which set their own cookies under their own policies.

How to control cookies

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking some cookies may affect parts of this site or stop affiliate partners from crediting a referral. You can also use your browser's private mode.
