New to affiliate marketing or blogging? Start with a simple plan instead of buying lots of tools.

Step 1: Understand affiliate marketing

You may earn a commission when someone completes a qualifying purchase or action through your tracked link. Programme rules and eligibility vary. Read the affiliate marketing basics and the South Africa guide.

Step 2: Choose one audience

Pick a topic you can help with, and write down five real questions that audience asks. choosing your niche can help.

Step 3: Choose a publishing channel that fits your budget

If you start a blog, compare the full cost of a domain, hosting and renewals before you commit. See our beginner blogging guide and web hosting in South Africa.

Step 4: Create useful content before you focus on sales

Explain who a product suits, what its limits are and what the alternatives are.

Step 5: Research suitable affiliate programmes

Confirm South African eligibility, payment options, minimum payouts and promotion rules directly with each provider.

Step 6: Measure what happens

Review your search impressions, clicks and affiliate activity, then improve the pages that already attract interest. our Analytics and Search Console guide shows how to set up the tools.
