Welcome to Kapya Market

My name is Joseph Kapya, and I am the creator of Kapya Market, a South African digital education platform. I created this website to help beginners understand online business, website creation, affiliate marketing, web hosting and modern technology.

My goal is to explain these topics in clear, practical language, with examples and information that are relevant to people starting their online journey in South Africa.

My background

I have a diploma in commercial administration and technology, as well as a certificate in cybersecurity. My interests and work include IT, content creation and affiliate marketing.

This combination of business and technology knowledge shapes the topics I cover. I believe that building an online presence involves more than making money: it also means choosing suitable tools, understanding technology and taking website security seriously.

What you will find on Kapya Market

  • Website creation guides covering domains, hosting and website builders.
  • Online store guides for people interested in selling products online.
  • Affiliate marketing guides explaining how affiliate programmes work and how beginners can get started.
  • Technology and cybersecurity information to help readers make more informed decisions online.

How Kapya Market makes money

Some articles contain affiliate links. This means that if you click a link and make a qualifying purchase, Kapya Market may earn a commission at no additional cost to you.

Affiliate relationships do not guarantee that a product or service is the right choice for every reader. We aim to provide useful information so you can compare options and make your own informed decisions.
